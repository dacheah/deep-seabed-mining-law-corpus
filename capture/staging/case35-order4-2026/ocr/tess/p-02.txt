List of Cases No. 35 2 Order 2026/4
Having regard to the Request submitted by TOML Registry on 5 June 2026
for the prescription of provisional measures by the Chamber in accordance with
article 290, paragraph 1, of the Convention,
Having ascertained the views of the Parties,
Fixes 2 July 2026 as the date for the opening of the oral proceedings;
Reserves the subsequent procedure for further decision.
Done in English and in French, both texts being equally authoritative, in the
Free and Hanseatic City of Hamburg, this tenth day of June two thousand and
twenty-six, in three copies, one of which will be placed in the archives of the Tribunal
and the others transmitted to the TOML and the Authority, respectively.
